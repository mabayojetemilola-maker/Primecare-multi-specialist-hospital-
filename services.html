Our services

Primary care · Maternity · Surgery · Diagnostics · Emergency · Pharmacy · Wellness

At Primecare Multi-Specialist Hospital we offer a wide range of medical services under one roof. Our goal is to make quality healthcare accessible, coordinated and compassionate for every patient who walks through our doors.

From the first consultation through diagnosis, treatment and follow-up, our team works together to support your recovery and long-term wellbeing.

P

Primary & Chronic Care

Comprehensive care for everyday health concerns and ongoing medical conditions.

  • General medical consultations
  • Chronic disease management (hypertension, diabetes and more)
  • Preventive health screening
  • Follow-up and long-term care plans
  • Health education and lifestyle advice
M

Maternal & Child Health

Dedicated support for mothers and children throughout pregnancy, birth and early childhood.

  • Antenatal care (ANC)
  • Safe delivery and postnatal care
  • Paediatric consultations
  • Vaccination and immunisation
  • Child wellness and growth monitoring
S

Surgical Specialities

Surgical care delivered with careful planning, skilled technique and attentive recovery support.

  • General Surgery
  • Urology
  • ENT
  • Obstetrics & Gynaecology
  • Pre- & Post-operative Care
  • Surgical consultations
I

Internal Medicine

Specialist assessment and management of adult medical conditions.

  • Adult internal medicine
  • Management of complex medical conditions
  • Infectious disease care
  • Specialist medical review
  • Coordinated treatment plans
D

Diagnostic Services

Reliable testing that helps guide accurate diagnosis and timely treatment.

  • Medical laboratory tests
  • Ultrasound scanning
  • Other diagnostic investigations
  • Clear explanation of results
  • Support for follow-up care
E

Emergency Care

Prompt response when urgent medical attention is needed.

  • 24/7 emergency services
  • Stabilisation and urgent care
  • Rapid assessment and triage
  • Referral coordination when required
Rx

Pharmacy

Quality medicines and clear guidance on how to use them safely.

  • Dispensing of prescribed medicines
  • Quality pharmaceutical products
  • Patient counselling on medications
  • Support for chronic medication needs
N

Nutrition & Wellness

Practical advice to support recovery, energy and long-term health.

  • Nutrition consultation
  • Wellness advice
  • Lifestyle and diet guidance
  • Support for recovery and prevention
V

Vaccination & Immunisation

Protecting children and adults against preventable diseases.

  • Routine childhood immunisation
  • Adult vaccination
  • Travel and special vaccines (where available)
  • Clear immunisation records
