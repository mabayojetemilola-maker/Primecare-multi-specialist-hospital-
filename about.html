About us

Primecare Multi-Specialist Hospital · Your Health, Our Priority

Primecare Multi-Specialist Hospital is a patient-centred healthcare facility in Onitsha dedicated to providing accessible, high-quality medical care for individuals and families. Our tagline — Your Health, Our Priority — guides every decision we make.

We bring together primary care, maternal and child health, surgical specialities, internal medicine, diagnostics, emergency services, pharmacy, vaccination and wellness support under one coordinated system of care.

Who we are

We are a multi-specialist hospital committed to clinical excellence and genuine compassion. Our team of doctors, nurses and support staff work together to deliver care that is thorough, respectful and responsive to each patient’s needs.

From the moment you arrive, our focus is on making you feel informed, supported and safe — whether you are visiting for a routine check-up, maternity care, surgery or emergency treatment.

Our promise

Expert medical team — qualified professionals focused on your recovery and wellbeing.

Safe & clean environment — high standards of hygiene and infection control.

24/7 care — emergency and inpatient services available around the clock.

Modern facilities — equipped for diagnostics, surgery, maternity and general care.

Patient-centred care — your comfort, dignity and outcome guide everything we do.

What we stand for

We believe that quality healthcare should not feel distant or intimidating. At Primecare we aim to create an atmosphere of trust: clear explanations, careful listening, and treatment plans that respect your circumstances and preferences.

Our location at 45B Awka Road, beside Chicken Republic in Onitsha, makes us easy to reach for residents of the city and nearby communities. We welcome new and returning patients seeking reliable multi-specialist care.
